# Vision

> **Insert a CD. Get a perfect copy in your own library.**
>
> 放进 CD，得到一份完美的副本，存进你自己的曲库。

CDR is for people who buy CDs and keep their own music library (Jellyfin,
Navidrome, a folder of FLAC). It covers the whole path from the disc to the
library: nobody should need to know about offsets, C2 errors, tag formats or
folder layouts to get there.

## What we promise

- **Accurate.** Every track comes with evidence: checked against
  AccurateRip, with a log anyone can re-check. When a rip isn't certain, CDR
  says which tracks to check; it never claims a perfect rip it can't prove.
- **Fast.** At the same accuracy, never slower than XLD's default settings.
  Speed never comes at the cost of accuracy.
- **Intuitive.** The defaults are right. Insert, confirm the album, rip, and it
  shows up in the library. Expert options live in Settings, out of the way.

## What it's for

Music you bought is yours, not rented from a platform. CDR writes open,
standard files (FLAC, standard tags, Picard-style names) that any player can
read and that will still work in ten years.

## How to use this

When a choice is unclear, pick what brings the user closer to a perfect copy
in their library with less to understand. A feature that makes the user
learn ripping jargon needs a strong reason.
